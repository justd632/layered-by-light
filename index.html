How ordering works

Because every piece is made to order, nothing is printed until you have seen it and approved it.

  1. Choose and personalise

    Pick your piece and enter the name, verse and finish you would like. Add a photo if your piece allows one.

  2. Pay by PayNow

    Scan the code at checkout, then give us your reference number and a screenshot so we can match your payment to your order.

  3. Approve your proof

    We send you a mock-up showing exactly how your piece will look. Nothing is printed until you say yes.

  4. Printed and posted

    Your piece is printed, finished by hand, and sent to you or straight to the person receiving it.

Free shipping over $25

Orders of $25 or more ship free anywhere in Singapore. Below that, shipping is a flat $2 — so it is often worth adding a bookmark or a keychain.

Ordering as a gift?

Tell us at checkout and we will send it directly, with your note hand-written on a card and no prices anywhere in the parcel.

If you need it by a particular date, say so on the order form and we will confirm before taking payment.
