Good to know

Questions, answered

How long will my order take?

Lead time is shown on each product page and starts from the day you approve your proof, not the day you order. Most pieces take 3–7 working days to make, plus delivery.

How do I pay?

By PayNow. When you submit your order you will get an order reference and payment instructions. Your order is confirmed once payment is received.

What is a proof?

A mock-up showing exactly how your piece will look — your name, your verse, your chosen font. We send it before printing and wait for you to approve it. If something is wrong, we fix it and send another.

Can I change my order after paying?

Yes, up until you approve your proof. After approval the piece goes into production and cannot be changed.

Can I return a personalised piece?

Personalised pieces cannot be returned or exchanged once you have approved your proof, since they cannot be resold. This is exactly why we ask you to check the proof carefully. If your piece arrives damaged or is not what you approved, contact us and we will put it right.

What photos work best?

Bright photos with good contrast and a plain background. Faces should be large in the frame. Dark, blurry or very busy photos do not render well through a lit panel — if yours will not work, we will tell you before printing rather than after.

Do you deliver?

Yes, across Singapore. Self-collection may be available by arrangement. Delivery cost is confirmed with your payment instructions.

Can you make something that is not in the shop?

Often, yes. Send us your idea with as much detail as you can and we will tell you if it is possible and what it would cost.
