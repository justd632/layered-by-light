About

A small workshop, one layer at a time

Layered by Light began with a simple idea: that the words people return to again and again deserve to live somewhere other than a screen.

Every piece is designed, printed and finished by hand in Singapore. Nothing is mass-produced and nothing sits in a warehouse — each order is made after it is placed, for the person it is meant for.

If you have an idea that is not in the shop, ask. Most of what is here started as somebody's request.
